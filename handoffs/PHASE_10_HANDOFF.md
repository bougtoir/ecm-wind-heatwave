# PHASE 10 HANDOFF — Siting optimization

## Status: SKIPPED (pre-specified condition not met)

Phase-9 aggregate WERR is small and not robustly positive (−0.028 [−0.04,−0.02]);
regional heterogeneity exists but the deployable effect (a few 1e-4 °C/MW on
North-Sea corridors) is ~2 orders of magnitude below decision-relevant demand
sensitivity. A siting optimization layer would therefore over-claim a signal the
data do not robustly support. Documented as a negative-per-design outcome.
