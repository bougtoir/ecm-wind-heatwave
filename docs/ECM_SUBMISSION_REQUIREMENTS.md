# ECM submission requirements (checked against Elsevier ECM Guide for Authors, Sept 2026)

- Article type: Original research / Full Length Article. No fixed main-text
  word cap in the current public guide (our ~2.4k-word body is well within
  any reading of the limit).
- Abstract: required; ours 217 words.
- Highlights: REQUIRED, 3–5 bullets, max 85 characters each — ours: 5
  bullets, all ≤85 chars (submission/highlights.txt + manuscript section).
- Graphical abstract: encouraged; separate file; min proportional size
  531(h)×1328(w) px; TIFF/EPS/PDF/MS Office preferred — ours PNG 2152×891
  (proportionally larger; PNG acceptable via EM upload; PDF version in
  figs/graphical_abstract.pdf also supplied).
- Single anonymized review: author-identifying lines are placeholders.
- Figures: embedded in the single-file manuscript ("Your Paper Your Way")
  plus separate PNG files and an editable PPTX supplied.
- References: numbered in order of appearance (Vancouver style), all cited.
- Declarations: funding, competing interests, data availability, code
  availability, CRediT present.
- AI disclosure: generative-AI assistance should be declared at submission
  (checklist note for the author).
- Cover letter: supplied.
