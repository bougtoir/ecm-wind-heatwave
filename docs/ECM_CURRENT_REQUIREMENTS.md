# ECM (Energy Conversion and Management) — Current Requirements Audit

Source: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/energy-conversion-and-management/publish/guide-for-authors
Audited: 2026-09-24 (UTC). Re-verify immediately before submission.

## Journal identity
- Energy Conversion and Management, Elsevier, ISSN 0196-8904. Impact Factor 10.9, CiteScore 19.8.
- Open-access companion: *Energy Conversion and Management: X* (same scope/team).
- Single-anonymized peer review; typically ≥2 reviewers.

## Aims & scope (verbatim essence)
Interdisciplinary original research on all important energy topics: energy generation,
utilization, conversion, storage, transmission, conservation, management, sustainability —
all energy types and resources including wind. Priority to interdisciplinary subjects using
more than one of: modeling, experimental, analysis, optimization, with appropriate verification.
Welcomes contributions advancing UN SDG 7.

## Article types
- **Full Length Article**: ≤ **9,000 words**; combined total of **≤ 15 tables + figures**.
- Review: 12,000 words max, invited only.

## Required submission items
- Abstract ≤ **250 words** (purpose, principal results, major conclusions).
- Keywords.
- **Highlights**: mandatory; short bullet collection of novel results + new methods
  (Elsevier standard: max 85 characters per bullet incl. spaces; separate file).
- **Graphical abstract**: required where applicable (single concise pictorial summary,
  separate file).
- Title page: corresponding author with email + full postal address.
- All figures with captions; all tables with titles/descriptions/footnotes; figure/table
  citations in text must match files provided.
- Competing interests statement mandatory even if none ("Declarations of interest: none"),
  plus Elsevier declarations tool.
- Funding sources declaration.
- Declaration of generative AI use, if applicable.
- Data statement (research data availability). Data linking/co-submission encouraged.
- Cover letter.
- Spell/grammar check; references cited in text ↔ list consistency.
- "Your Paper Your Way": new submissions may be a single Word or PDF file; correct
  formatting required only at revision stage.

## Policy points relevant to this project
- Generative AI: use must be declared; AI cannot be an author; AI use in figures/artwork restricted.
- Research data: data availability statement expected; repository linking encouraged →
  our public repo + release must back this.
- No authorship changes after acceptance — author list must be final at submission.
- Subscription (no APC) and Open Access options both available; OA page linked from guide.

## Implications for this manuscript (design constraints)
1. Target Full Length Article: ≤9,000 words incl. everything per Elsevier counting norms;
   ≤15 tables+figures combined → plan ~8-10 figures + 2-4 tables in main text; rest in supplement.
2. Abstract ≤250 words; Highlights (≤85 chars/bullet, 3-5 bullets); graphical abstract needed.
3. Must foreground energy-conversion/management contribution (WERR/E_net) — a pure
   meteorology framing risks desk rejection.
4. Data/code availability statement pointing to public repo required.
5. Declarations: competing interests, funding, AI-use declaration.
